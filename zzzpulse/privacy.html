ZzzPulse: White Noise & Sleep – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

What the app does

ZzzPulse generates white, pink and brown noise on your device. The sound is created locally in real time. Nothing you play is uploaded, recorded or shared.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any personal data. The app has no account system. It keeps a few settings (sound type, volume, timer) in private storage on your device. This data never leaves the device.

Advertising

The app may show ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you are asked for consent through a Google consent form, and you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy button in the app. See how Google uses data. You can also reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings.

Permissions

Internet: only to load ads. Notifications: to show playback controls while sound is playing. Foreground service: to keep the sound playing with the screen off. The app does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, files or location.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.

Contact

Questions: contact the developer through the GitHub profile github.com/shimsh2.
