Luxly: Light Meter & Lux Meter – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What the app does

Luxly measures ambient light using your device's built-in light sensor and shows the result on screen. Measurements are processed on your device only. They are not uploaded, stored on a server, or shared.

Data we collect

We (the developer) do not collect, store or receive any personal data. The app has no account system. It keeps a few settings (such as your unit preference and a launch counter) in private storage on your device. This data never leaves the device.

Advertising

The app shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location and app interaction data to serve and measure ads. Where required (for example in the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom) you are asked for consent through a Google consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy & ads choices. See how Google uses data. You can also reset or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's Android settings.

Permissions

The app uses the internet only to load ads. The light sensor requires no permission. The app does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location.

Children

The app is intended for a general audience and is not directed to children under 13.
